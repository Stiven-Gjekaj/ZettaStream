# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to the other people here. Speak about code, not about the person who
wrote it.

## What is expected

- Speak with courtesy, and accept that other people know things you do not.
- Give criticism about the work. Take criticism about your work.
- Accept a decision when it is made, even when you argued for a different one.
- Remember that most people here give their time for nothing.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, and attacks on a person rather than on their work.
- Sexual language or images, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Comments that attack a person for who they are, which includes their age,
  body, disability, ethnic group, sex, gender, level of experience, nationality,
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.
- Publishing private information about a person without their permission.
- Behaviour that a reasonable person would find unwelcome in a place of work.

## Where this applies

This code applies in every space of the project, which includes the issues, the
pull requests, the discussions, and the commit messages. It also applies when a
person speaks for the project in a public place.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer at stivenagostingjekaj@gmail.com. Every
report is read. The person who reports a problem keeps their privacy.

The maintainer will answer within a few days, and will say what happens next. A
report that concerns the maintainer has no good answer inside this project, and
GitHub accepts a report of its own at https://github.com/contact/report-abuse.

## What happens after a report

The maintainer decides what to do, and the answer fits the behaviour. It can be
a private word, a public correction, the removal of a comment, or a block from
the project. The maintainer is not required to explain a decision in public.

## Note

This document is written for this project. It follows the same idea as the
Contributor Covenant, but it is not that text and it is shorter.
